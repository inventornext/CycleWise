CycleWise

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

CycleWise is a period tracker app developed by Nivedita Kumari and Ganesh Kumar Pandey ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: your cycle data stays on your phone, and we never see it.

Information you enter

You can record period dates, flow, pain, symptoms, mood, notes and app settings such as language and PIN. All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is not uploaded to any server, and we have no way to access it.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, email or phone number.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services that collect your data.
  • We do not sell, share or rent any information to anyone.

Purchases

If CycleWise offers an optional paid unlock, payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase is valid. We do not receive your card details or link your purchase to your health data.

Exports and doctor reports

When you export your data or create a PDF report, the file is created on your phone. It is shared only if you choose to share it, and only with the app or person you pick.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. Discreet mode hides what a reminder is about so others cannot see it on your screen.

Deleting your data

You can delete all your data from the app settings, or by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. Because we do not hold a copy, nothing remains with us.

Children and teens

CycleWise includes a Teen mode with age-appropriate content and no fertility features. The app does not collect personal information from any user, including teens.

Medical disclaimer

CycleWise gives general information, not medical advice. Predictions are estimates and must not be used as birth control. Please see a doctor for any health concern.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through GitHub.
