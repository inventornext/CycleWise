# CycleWise

**A private period tracker for teens and adults, in English and Hindi.**

No account. No ads. Your cycle data stays on your phone.

🌐 Website: https://inventornext.github.io/cyclewise
📱 Google Play: coming soon

## Features


- **Predictions that learn** from your own cycle history
- **Reminders** before your period, with a discreet mode
- **Know when to ask a doctor**: flags missed, long or heavy periods, severe pain and signs of PCOS
- **Symptom and mood tracking** with pattern insights
- **Doctor report**: one-page PDF for your gynaecologist
- **Teen mode** with a first-period guide, and ovulation estimates for adults
- **Learn** section with short, honest articles
- **English and Hindi**, switchable any time
- **PIN lock** and a hidden "My Calendar" icon

## Built on medical guidelines

Normal ranges, health alerts and articles follow ACOG Committee Opinions 651 and 760, FIGO 2018, the 2023 International PCOS Guideline, NICE NG88, Wilcox et al. (NEJM 1995) and Bull et al. (npj Digital Medicine 2019). The full list is in the app under Learn.

## Privacy

All data is stored on the device. CycleWise has no servers, no analytics and no ads. Read the full [privacy policy](https://inventornext.github.io/cyclewise/privacy.html).

## Disclaimer

CycleWise gives general information, not medical advice. Predictions must not be used as birth control.
